# Film Budget Readiness Review

Private research draft. Do not publish or offer this service until relevant reviewer experience is substantiated.

## Draft promise

Before paying for a full line-producer package, get a structured review of the draft budget you plan to use for a film grant or serious financing conversation.

## Proposed fixed scope — $249

- Review one draft top sheet and detailed budget for one project
- Read the project's one-page summary and stated production assumptions
- Flag missing cost categories, unsupported rates, inconsistent units, weak contingencies, and totals that do not reconcile
- Return a missing-cost register and prioritized question list
- Identify assumptions that require confirmation from a line producer, accountant, union, insurer, lawyer, grant body, or local production office
- One 30-minute handoff call
- One clarification round within seven days

## Explicit boundaries

This is not a production-ready budget, shooting schedule, grant-writing service, legal or accounting advice, union-compliance opinion, tax-credit analysis, insurance quote, or guarantee of funding. It does not replace a qualified line producer or production accountant.

## Buyer outcome

The buyer should leave knowing which numbers are supported, which important costs are absent, which assumptions can materially change the total, and which questions must be answered before commissioning a full budget and schedule.

## Qualification

A fit has a draft budget, a project summary, a target budget level, a known jurisdiction, and a real grant or financing milestone. A poor fit needs the budget built from a screenplay, wants current union or tax guidance, or needs a schedule and financing plan.

## Evidence still required

- Credible reviewer experience with real production budgets
- Direct confirmation that filmmakers will buy review-only work instead of a low-cost full budget
- A jurisdiction-neutral checklist that does not present unstable rates or rules as authoritative

Until those conditions are met, keep this as research rather than a sellable claim.
